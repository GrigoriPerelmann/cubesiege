    Weapon Power Index

    100 is a stock pistol. Weapons run left to right in unlock order, so each row should get steadily stronger towards the right.

    WPI = single-target DPS0.3 × crowd DPS0.3 × sustained DPS0.4. Sustained DPS covers a 60 s fight with one ammo crate every 20 s, falling back to the pistol when the gun runs dry. Hover a cell for the parts.

    Field test
